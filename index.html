AMPHOREUS

Amphoreus, The Eternal Land is a segmentum hidden deep in the cosmos. It is severed from the outside and is difficult for anyone to detect or encounter. Standard interstellar navigation can barely perceive its existence, let alone pass through or arrive at the world itself.

Amphoreus is the fifth accessible planet after Penacony in Honkai: Star Rail. This world is only accessible through "Garden's of Recollection Mirror". Amphoreus is a sandbox world by Scepter δ-m113 (Irontomb) under Lygus's administration. The Astral Express continues to initiate their trailblazing journey here by trying to uncover the truth behind the Three Paths.

Upon the Astral Express's arrival, Amphoreus is already at the verge of destruction. Not long after, they meet the Chrysos Heirs, heroes whom they'll be fighting alongside throughout their journey on Amphoreus.
