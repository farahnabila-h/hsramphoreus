ABILITIES

Hundred Rockets

Basic Attack

[Single Target]

Toughness Break: 10

Deals Quantum DMG equal to 15%/30% of Tribbie's Max HP to one designated enemy. Deals Quantum DMG equal to 7.5%/15% of Tribbie's Max HP to adjacent targets.

Where'd the Gifts Go

Skill

[Support]

Gains "Numinosity," lasting for 3 turn(s). This duration decreases by 1 at the start of this unit's every turn. While Tribbie has "Numinosity," increases all ally targets' All-Type RES PEN by 12%/24%.

Guess Who Lives Here

Ultimate

[AoE]

Toughness Reduction: 20

Activates a Zone and deals Quantum DMG equal to 15% /30% of Tribbie's Max HP to all enemies. While the Zone lasts, increases enemy targets' DMG taken by 15%/30%. After an ally target attacks, for every 1 target hit, deals 1 instance of Quantum Additional DMG equal to 6%/12% of Tribbie's Max HP to the target that has the highest HP among the hit targets. The Zone lasts for 2 turn(s). This duration decreases by 1 at the start of this unit's every turn.

Beginning of the End

Technique

[Enhance]

After using Technique and upon entering battle, obtains "Numinosity," lasting for 3 turn(s).

Busy as Tribbie

Talent

[AoE]

Toughness Reduction: 5

After other ally characters use Ultimate, Tribbie launches Follow-up ATK, dealing Quantum DMG equal to 9%/18% of Tribbie's Max HP to all enemies. This effect triggers up to 1 time per character. When Tribbie uses Ultimate, resets the trigger count for other ally characters. If the target was defeated before the Follow-up ATK is launched, then launches the Follow-up ATK against new enemy targets entering the battlefield.

ASCENSION 2

Lamb Outside the Wall...

After using Talent's Follow-up ATK, increases the DMG dealt by Tribbie by 72%. This effect can stack up to 3 time(s), lasting for 3 turn(s).

ASCENSION 4

Glass Ball with Wings!

While the Zone lasts, Tribbie's Max HP increases by an amount equal to 9% of the sum of all ally characters' Max HP.

ASCENSION 6

Pebble at Crossroads?

At the start of battle, Tribbie regenerates 30 Energy. After other ally targets attack, Tribbie regenerates 1.5 Energy for each target hit.
